Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Access

videoremotion uses TikTok Login Kit (user.info.basic) and the TikTok Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish) solely to authenticate your TikTok account and upload videos at your explicit request.

2. How Data Is Stored

OAuth 2.0 access tokens and refresh tokens are stored exclusively on your local machine (in your local configuration file) and are never transmitted to, stored on, or shared with any external third-party servers.

3. Data Usage

We do not sell, rent, analytics-track, or share your personal data or TikTok account data with any third parties. Tokens are used strictly to communicate directly with official TikTok API endpoints (open.tiktokapis.com).

4. Data Deletion & Revocation

You can delete stored OAuth tokens at any time by clicking "Disconnect TikTok" in the studio settings panel or by revoking access in the TikTok mobile app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
